📊 BlinkIT Grocery Sales Analysis

Overview

•	This project presents an end-to-end Exploratory Data Analysis (EDA) of BlinkIT grocery sales dataset.

•	The objective of this analysis is to understand sales performance, identify key revenue drivers, and extract meaningful business insights using Python.

•	The focus of this project is not just visualization, but structured analysis and business-oriented interpretation.

________________________________________
🎯 Objectives

•	Analyze overall sales distribution

•	Understand the impact of outlet type and location tier

•	Examine the relationship between product ratings and sales

•	Identify high-performing item categories

•	Generate practical business recommendations

________________________________________
🔎 Key Insights

•	The correlation between product rating and individual product sales is very weak, indicating that rating alone is not a strong predictor of product-level sales performance.

•	However, aggregated analysis shows that high-rated products contribute a larger share of total revenue.

•	Outlet type significantly influences total revenue, with certain formats outperforming others.

•	Sales performance varies across location tiers, highlighting the importance of geographic segmentation.

•	A limited number of item categories contribute disproportionately to total revenue.
________________________________________
💡 Business Recommendations

•	Focus on high-performing outlet types, as they contribute significantly to overall revenue. Future expansion strategies can prioritize similar formats.

•	Maintain product quality and customer satisfaction, since high-rated products contribute a larger share of total revenue at an aggregated level.

•	Optimize inventory allocation toward top-performing item categories to maximize revenue contribution.

•	Analyze underperforming outlet tiers or categories to identify potential operational or pricing improvements.

•	Use data-driven monitoring of sales trends to regularly evaluate performance across outlet types and locations.
